Summary

Tip-In Events (Lean Spikes)

Throttle opening events sorted by lean spike severity. Large lean spikes indicate fuel film primary values are too low for that MAP transition zone.

Tip-Out Events (Rich Overshoot)

Throttle closing events sorted by rich overshoot. Persistent rich after tip-out indicates fuel film decrease time constant is too slow.
