Firmware I/O Channels

FIRMWARE0–FIRMWARE31: virtual I/O channels the ECU passes values through. From writes the channel (a CAN receive signal or an output); To reads it (a switch or sensor input assigned to it); Then used by lists the settings that point at that input. Click anything to follow it.

My Labels

Your own notes on settings and I/O resources — what an auxiliary output, driver switch or FIRMWARE channel is used for. A label belongs to the setting's name, so it shows on every tune version and firmware revision that has it until you change it. Add or edit one from the Follow window (click a setting's name).
