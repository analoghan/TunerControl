Ignition Review — Help

In the Normal ignition state the M1 runs the table plus the compensations (MoTeC training 50: Ignition Timing should equal the table under normal conditions; a difference means a compensation is in play). The review rebuilds that from the tune and the log, and accounts for every degree:

  • Table = Ignition Timing Main + (Alternative Fuel Ignition Timing − Main) × Alternative Fuel Ignition Timing Blend at Fuel Composition, looked up at Engine Speed × Engine Load Normalised. With Load Normalised Mode = Inlet Manifold Pressure, load is MAP in kPa. The Z slice is the one Driver Ignition Timing Main Switch selects (its Default mapping when no switch is wired).
  • Compensation = the logged Ignition Timing Compensation, and separately every non-zero "… Ignition Timing Compensation / Trim" table in the tune whose inputs are logged (inlet air temperature, coolant…), to show which table it comes from.

Log Ignition Timing, Ignition Timing Compensation, Ignition Timing State, Fuel Composition, Inlet Manifold Pressure, Inlet Air Temperature and the eight Ignition Cylinder N Knock Level channels. Ignition Cylinder N Knock Trim (20 Hz) shows knock control's retard per cylinder.

Findings

  • Ignition Timing follows the tune — median and 90th percentile of logged − (table + compensation) in the Normal state. A large difference means a different tune ran the log, or something not modelled sets timing.
  • Timing below / above the tune — Normal-state stretches of 0.3 s or more at least 1° off, at a steady load (MAP moved less than 5 kPa over ±0.2 s, since MAP is logged at 10 Hz and timing at 20 Hz). Below usually means knock control, a torque reduction or a limit.
  • Compensation — how much the compensations took at cruise and under boost (≥ 100 kPa), whether the tune's tables explain the logged value, and what inlet air temperature took out under boost.
  • Flex fuel — the blend at the logged composition, Alternative − Main where the log ran, and boost rows where the Alternative Fuel table runs less advance than Main. Ethanol cools the charge and resists knock: MoTeC training 67 starts ethanol on the petrol timing, verifies fuelling, then typically finds 6–8° more at the same boost, with the knock sensors watching.
  • Cylinder trims — Ignition Cylinder N Trim Main against the knock input each cylinder is on (Knock Cylinder N Resource). Trims only on one input's cylinders while the other input reads several times lower is flagged: those cylinders may need the same and their sensor can't show it.
  • Knock margin — cells where the loudest cylinder's 99th-percentile knock level reaches 80 % of Knock Threshold.
  • Ignition Timing State Limit Advance / Limit Retard / Check Timing are reported when they occur.

Timing by Cell

Normal-state running binned to the nearest Ignition Timing Main site; a cell needs 0.5 s. Each shows the median logged timing, the compensation in it, and the knock level as a share of the threshold (green under 50 %, amber 50–80 %, red 80 % or more). Hover a cell for the table value and Alternative − Main.

The knock margin is only as good as the knock inputs: a sensor that reads low makes its cylinders look quiet. Check the Knock Analyzer's sensor balance and calibration before reading green cells as room for more timing. This page never suggests adding timing.
