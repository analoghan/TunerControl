Pull Report — Help

A pull is at least 0.8 s with Inlet Manifold Pressure ≥ 100 kPa or Throttle Pedal ≥ 85 %, above 1500 rpm, with the engine running ≥ 30 s (gaps under 0.4 s are joined). On a supercharged engine that also catches boost at part pedal; those are marked as not full-throttle. Choose several logs to see all their pulls in one table. The tune is optional: it gives Knock Threshold, so knock is judged against it.

What each pull shows

  • Lambda — median per bank past the first 0.5 s (the tip-in), and the most lean against the aim 0.3 s earlier (the lambda delay measured on this car), held 0.2 s. Flagged at 0.03 λ leaner (0.05 λ = fail). Banks more than 0.03 λ apart are flagged. Closed-loop trims of 5 % or more are noted: the fuel model is off there (VE Table Tuning).
  • Knock — each cylinder's peak (held 50 ms) against Knock Threshold at that rpm: 80 % = check, 100 % = fail. Knock trims, when logged, show retard per cylinder.
  • Timing — minimum and median Ignition Timing; Ignition Timing State other than Normal is noted.
  • Fuel supply — rail pressure under aim (held 0.2 s) beyond the larger of 10 bar and 10 % of the aim, with the pump control reached; low-side pressure below 250 kPa (fail) or dropping 50 kPa from the second before the pull; Fuel Pump 2 state; injector duty ≥ 85 %.
  • Injection — the start of injection (Fuel Cylinder 1 Primary Output Pulse Angle 1) and its end (start minus the pulse's crank degrees, from the pulse width and rpm). Flagged: an end under 10° before the spark (5° = fail), an end after Fuel Timing Primary Limit Main (the pulse is cut short), and a start before the exhaust valve closes. The exhaust closing uses the DI Injection Timing page's default cam (229/244° at 0.050", 118 LSA, 114 ICL; seat to seat = +52°), moved by the logged inlet cam advance — those cam figures are not confirmed for this engine. Noted: the start reaching Injection Start Limit (the M1 wanted an earlier start) or the end running into the compression stroke. With the angle logged at 1 Hz a pull has only a few start readings.
  • Temperatures — inlet air at 60 °C or more is flagged (heat-soaked charge); charge, coolant and oil temperature are listed.
  • Oil pressure — checked against 10 psi (69 kPa) per 1000 rpm, held 0.2 s.
  • Electrical, limits, cuts — battery under 12.5 V, Throttle Limit or Engine Speed Limit State active, Ignition Cut, a change in Fuel Output Cut Count outside overrun.

Across pulls lists each kind of flag once, with its worst case and how many pulls had it. Show opens a pull's flags, all its numbers and the log around it.

For a pull worth tuning from: warm engine, 3rd gear, from about 2500 rpm to the shift point, pedal to the floor, on a closed road or a dyno. Log lambda, MAP, pedal and rail pressure fast (50 Hz), injector duty and Pulse Angle at 10–20 Hz and the knock trims at 20 Hz.
