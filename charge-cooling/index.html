Aim Steps in the Log

Every change of Fuel Mixture Aim by 0.03 or more between two stretches where it holds still for 3 s. Lambda is averaged over the last 2 s before the step and from 1.5 s after it (the sensor's delay); tracking compares the change in lambda with the change in aim, so an error at the starting point cancels out.
