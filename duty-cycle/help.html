Injector Duty Cycle — Help

Shows how close the injectors come to their limit across rpm and load, and how much of the load range the log actually covers. Results are in seconds and in stretches that last a while, not single samples, so one glitch can't make the injectors look maxed out.

Quick Start

  1. Choose a MoTeC log (.ld or CSV) with Engine Speed and Fuel Injector Primary Duty Cycle (Secondary Duty Cycle is used if there is no primary). Inlet Manifold Pressure and Fuel Cylinder 1 Primary Output Pulse Width 1 add the load coverage and the injection window.
  2. Set the warning and critical levels (default 80 % and 90 %).
  3. Click Analyze.

What Duty Cycle Means on a DI M1

On a port-injected engine, duty cycle is the share of the whole 720° engine cycle the injector is open. On a direct-injection M1 the logged Fuel Injector Primary Duty Cycle is a share of the injection window instead: the crank angle the injection is allowed to use. Dividing the pulse in crank degrees by the duty cycle comes out at an almost constant angle, which the tool reports as the implied injection window. 100 % means the pulse fills the window, not that the injector is open all the time.

So there are three ways to get headroom back at the top end:

  • A longer window: start injection earlier, within the valve events (see DI Injection Timing).
  • More rail pressure: the same fuel goes in with a shorter pulse, if the pump can hold it (see DI Fuel Pressure).
  • Larger injectors.

Results

Summary

  • Peak held 0.3 s: the highest duty cycle the injectors stayed at for 0.3 s, with the rpm (and MAP) where it happened. This is the number the status and advice use.
  • Engine running, time above warning and time above critical, in seconds.
  • Highest reached: the top rpm and MAP in the log. Duty cycle above these isn't measured.
  • Injection window (implied): median of pulse degrees ÷ duty cycle, shown once at least 100 samples have a usable pulse.

Duty Cycle by RPM

One row per 500 rpm band: time spent there, the 95th percentile, the peak held for 0.3 s (with the MAP there), the single highest sample, and a status (OK / WARNING / CRITICAL) from the held peak.

High Duty Cycle Episodes

Stretches above the warning level that last at least 0.3 s (gaps up to 0.2 s are joined), highest first, with time, length, rpm and MAP.

Recommendations

How the held peak compares with your levels, and how much load the log covers. Under 2 s above 100 kPa is flagged as too little boost to judge full-load headroom.

A cruise log can't show full-load headroom. Duty cycle rises with rpm and load, so log full-throttle pulls to near the rev limit (safely) before relying on the result.
Tip: if the secondary injectors are also used in the log, the tool says so; the numbers here cover the primary injectors.
