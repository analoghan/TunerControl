Injector Duty Cycle Monitor

Injector duty cycle across RPM and load, measured in seconds and sustained stretches (not single samples), to show how much headroom is left — and how much of the load range the log actually covers. On a direct-injection M1 the duty cycle is a share of the injection window, so 100 % means the pulse fills it.
