Cross-Tool Diagnostics — Help

Diagnostics reads one MoTeC M1 log and checks fuelling, knock and DI rail pressure together, so a problem that shows up in more than one place is reported once with its likely cause. For example, knock that only happens while the engine is lean points at fuelling, not timing. It is a quick screen: each finding names the tool to open for the detailed analysis.

Quick Start

  1. Choose a MoTeC log: the .ld file itself or a CSV export from i2.
  2. Optionally add the tune: a stored tune from the M1 Config Viewer (suggested from the log's ECU serial and date), or a .m1pkg file. With a tune, the injector findings use its Minimum Volume and Linearisation.
  3. Click Analyze All.

Channels Used

Everything is optional except Engine Speed; a check whose channels are missing is skipped.

  • Fuelling: Exhaust Lambda Bank 1 / Bank 2 (or Exhaust Lambda), Fuel Mixture Aim, Fuel Closed Loop Control Bank 1 / 2 Trim, Fuel Cylinder 1 Primary Output Pulse Width 1, Engine Overrun State, Inlet Manifold Pressure.
  • Rail pressure: Fuel Pressure Direct Bank 1 and its Aim (logged in MPa; shown in bar).
  • Knock: Ignition Cylinder 1–8 Knock Level.

Summary

TileWhat it counts
Log Duration / Total SamplesLength of the log. .ld logs are resampled to the fastest channel (100 Hz), so samples are not a measure of how much data there is — the counts below are episodes, not samples.
Lean Episodes (fuelling)Stretches of at least 0.3 s with lambda above 1.06 while the injectors kept firing — real lean running.
Injection Cuts (no overrun)Lean episodes where the injector pulse width went to zero without the overrun fuel cut (Engine Overrun State 2). On an M1 that is usually Fuel Injector Primary Minimum Volume switching all injectors off.
Knock EventsStretches where any cylinder's knock level is above 20 % (one event per stretch, not per sample).
Pressure Below AimShare of the running time with rail pressure more than 2 bar below its aim.
Injecting Below 1 msShare of injecting time with pulses under 1 ms, the DI injector's short-pulse (non-linear) region.

Lean episodes during the overrun fuel cut are expected (lambda reads air about 0.4 s after the cut) and are not counted as problems.

Findings

Findings are sorted critical → warning → info. Each says what was measured and what to check next.

FindingShown when
Knock Events Correlated with Lean FuelingMore than 5 knock events and over 15 % of them start while lambda is above 1.06 with the injectors firing. Fix the fuelling before pulling timing.
Injection Cut Without Overrun3 or more injection cuts without the overrun cut. With the tune, it compares the logged fuel volume with Minimum Volume and its Hysteresis.
Lean Episodes With Rail Pressure Below Aim3 or more lean-while-fuelling episodes with the rail more than 10 bar below aim.
Sustained CL Trim Offset — Bank 1 / 2The closed-loop trim averages more than ±5 % while closed loop is active. A trim that big everywhere points at the fuel model as a whole (injector reference flow, fuel pressure) rather than single VE cells.
Fuel Pressure Tracking IssueRail pressure is more than 2 bar below aim for over 15 % of the running time.
Knock Events Detected (Not Lean-Related)Knock events with none of them during lean running — timing-related knock.
Lean at Short Pulse WidthsAt least half of 3+ lean-while-fuelling episodes had pulses under 1 ms.
Linearisation Doesn't Cover Short PulsesWith a tune: more than 5 % of injecting time is under 1 ms but Fuel Injector Primary Linearisation has a single Fuel Volume site, so it can't correct short pulses.
No Cross-Domain Issues DetectedNone of the above.
Knock here uses a fixed 20 % level. A typical M1 Knock Threshold table runs 20–40 % of the knock level scale by rpm, so this is only a first look. The Knock Analyzer reads the tune's own Knock Threshold table, and its count is the one to act on.

Where to Go Next

  • Lean episodes, injection cuts, short pulses, Linearisation: Injector Characterization (Lean Spike Analysis, Injector Settings From the Tune).
  • Rail pressure: DI Fuel Pressure — whether it is a pump capacity limit (high rpm and load) or a control problem (everywhere).
  • Knock: Knock Analyzer — events per cylinder against the tune's threshold, and the RPM × load grid.
  • Trim offsets: VE Table Tuning once the injector data and fuel pressure are right.
Tip: a short log gives small counts, and the findings need several episodes before they say anything. Use a drive that covers idle, cruise, tip-ins and some load.
