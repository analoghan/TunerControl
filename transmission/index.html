Shifts by Transition

Medians per gear change. A shift runs from the request (Gear Target changes) to the Gear change: the delay before the ratio starts moving (the oncoming element filling and taking torque), then the ratio change. Flare is input speed above the gear it should be held to; sag and bump are the drop and rise of vehicle acceleration against the half second before the shift.
